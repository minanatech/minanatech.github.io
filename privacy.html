Privacy policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026. Developer/data controller: Minana Tech (developer of Luma White Icon Pack). Contact: minanatech@gmail.com.

Local application features

Luma White does not require a user account. Icons and wallpapers are included in the app. Favorites are stored on your device and can be deleted by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. The app does not operate a server for these features.

Installed applications and icon requests

To identify missing icons, the app reads applications that provide a launcher activity. This list stays on your device. When you select apps and choose to send an icon request, you can review the selected app names and package/activity identifiers. The app opens your email application; you decide whether to send the email. Your email provider handles transmission under its own privacy policy.

The developer receives your email address, message, and selected app details only if you send that email. These are used to respond and improve icon coverage. Requests are retained for up to 12 months unless needed to resolve an active support issue. Contact the support address to request deletion of received correspondence.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob to display banner ads and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage applicable privacy choices. Depending on your device, region, configuration, and choices, Google may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics, and approximate location inferred from IP for advertising, fraud prevention, and measurement. These services can transmit data even though the app's icon and wallpaper content is offline.

The app requests consent information on launch and only requests ads when Google's consent framework permits it. Where required, you can reopen privacy choices in Settings. You can also manage or delete your advertising ID through Android settings.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information. The app does not include Firebase Analytics or a separate application analytics service.

Children and international users

Luma White is intended for users aged 13 and above and is not directed to children. Advertising consent requirements can depend on local law. If you believe support correspondence includes a child's personal information, contact the developer.

Choices and security

You can use included content without sending icon requests. Website links, email, consent services, and ads may require an internet connection. External providers have their own retention and transfer practices. You may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict your personal data under applicable law; contact the developer for support correspondence and Google for data it controls.

Website hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process connection information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure their services. See GitHub's privacy statement. This site does not add analytics, advertising scripts, or tracking cookies.

Changes

The effective date will be updated when this policy changes. The current version will always be available at this address.
