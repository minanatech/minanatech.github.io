Make it yours

  1. Install Luma White and a compatible launcher.
  2. Open your launcher's icon settings and choose Luma White.
  3. Pair your icons with an included wallpaper.

Tested with Nova Launcher 8.0.18 and Lawnchair 15 Beta 3. Smart Launcher support is planned and still requires device verification. Stock launchers may not accept icon packs.

What's included

Offline icon browsing, search, favorites, wallpaper previews, and optional icon requests sent through your email app. The current test version includes 66 automatic app mappings. Other symbols are alternatives for manual selection through compatible launcher settings.
